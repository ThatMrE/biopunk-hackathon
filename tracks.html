Pick a lane.
Build something living.

Tracks drop at the prototype-weekend kickoff — often owned by a sponsor who supplies the challenge, the reagents, and a judge. Every track is scoped so a winning idea is buildable in one week at a BSL-1 bench. Teams pick one.

🎯

Sponsor-owned

A company owns a track: writes the challenge, funds the prize, supplies in-kind reagents, sends a mentor/judge. Past editions: Biosecurity by Pilgrim, workshops by Seanome.

🧪

One-week feasible

Prompts are framed so the winning design can be built from stock or rush parts and validated by demo day. A tight prompt keeps every team inside the week.

☣

BSL-1 by design

Every track lives inside the biosafety envelope: non-human, non-clinical, well-characterized organisms. The gate is baked into the challenge.

Track_menu

The vectors

A starter menu drawn from real Biopunk editions (Longevity × AI, Robotics, Biosecurity, DeSci, bio-imaging) plus the classic buildable synbio lanes. Adapt to your city, your sponsors, and a regional problem.

01

🧠 AI + Cognition

Merge silicon and grey matter. Neurofeedback loops, AI-assisted design and decision-making, cognitive amplifiers, brain–computer symbiosis. Where compute meets the mind.

Buildable in a week
▸ AI-assisted protein/sequence design → order → express▸ Neurofeedback or biosignal prototype▸ A software tool other teams can use
Sponsors: AWS · GCP · Modal (compute), Benchling · Special prize: Best Use of AI (disclosed)
02

⏳ Health + Longevity

Push the biological machine past its factory settings. Biometrics, longevity protocols, recovery optimization, aging biology, ECM imaging — the through-line of Biopunk's BioViz and Human Enhancement editions.

Buildable in a week
▸ A biometric/wearable readout▸ Real-time imaging/visualization rig▸ An aging-related reporter assay
Sponsors: Vitalist Bay, longevity funds · Special prize: Best Longevity Hack
03

🔬 Diagnostics + Biosensors

Engineer cells or cell-free systems that detect something and report it — a contaminant, a metabolite, a signal — with a colour or fluorescence you can read by eye or plate reader.

Buildable in a week
▸ Reporter constructs (GFP/RFP/lacZ)▸ Inducible promoter characterization▸ Cell-free paper-strip sensor
Sponsors: Twist, IDT, Benchling · Special prize: Best Biosensor
04

🧫 Biomanufacturing + Living Materials

Engineer a microbe to make a molecule — a pigment, flavour, fragrance, protein, or living material. Biohack mycelium, brew a dye, grow something that shouldn't exist yet.

Buildable in a week
▸ Pigment production (violacein, carotenoids)▸ Small-scale protein expression▸ Mycelium / living-material prototype
Sponsors: food-tech, fermentation & ingredients firms · Special prize: Best Bioproduct
05

🛰 DeSci + Open Science Tooling

From the Biopunk DeSci edition: citizen-led trials, remote wet-lab automation, peer-to-peer publishing, DAO-governed science, on-chain lab protocols, decentralized environmental sensing.

Buildable in a week
▸ An open protocol / provenance tool▸ Remote lab-automation demo▸ Environmental sensing rig
Sponsors: MuseMatrix, Causality Network · Special prize: Best DeSci Tool
06

🔧 Interfaces, Wearables & Open Hardware

New ways for meat to talk to metal — plus the tools that lower the barrier itself. Sensor rigs, experimental wearables, DIY bio hardware, low-cost lab instruments, robotics (40+ SO101 arms on tap).

Buildable in a week
▸ A sub-$50 instrument replacing a $300+ one▸ An OT-2 automation protocol▸ A wearable / haptic / robotics prototype
Sponsors: Opentrons, Oxford Nanopore, robotics partners · Special prize: Best Open Hardware
